Input column: urdu_text 
Target column: is_sarcastic, where 1 = sarcastic and 0 = non-sarcastic. 
The text may contain Urdu script, Roman/English tokens, emojis, punctuation, hashtags, 
mentions, URLs, spelling variation, and informal social-media writing. 
Inspect missing values, duplicates, label balance, and text length before modelling. Use a 
stratified train/validation/test split and avoid test-set leakage. 
 
Goal 
 
1. Exploratory analysis - report class distribution, text-length statistics, representative examples, 
and common data-quality issues. 
2. Urdu-aware preprocessing - design and justify normalization/tokenization. Do not blindly 
remove emojis, punctuation, repetition, hashtags, or code-mixed terms; test whether these are 
useful sarcasm cues. 
3. Classical baseline - train at least one TF-IDF based classifier (e.g., Logistic Regression, Linear 
SVM, or Naive Bayes). Word and/or character n-grams are allowed. 
4. Neural model - train at least one neural classifier (e.g., CNN, BiLSTM/GRU, or another 
justified architecture) using Keras/TensorFlow or PyTorch. 
5. Transformer experiment - fine-tune an Urdu-capable or multilingual pretrained transformer 
(e.g., an appropriate BERT/XLM-R family model) and compare it with the baseline and neural 
model. 
6. Ablation study - evaluate at least two preprocessing/feature choices, such as keeping vs. 
removing emojis, word vs. character features, or normalized vs. minimally processed text.

ambiguous labels where applicable.
